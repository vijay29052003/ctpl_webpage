Destination boards that tell every passenger where the bus is going and what comes next.

ITMS combines LED destination boards, an inside passenger information sign, and a driver controller with GPS-triggered voice announcements. It is built around IS 16490:2016 and the Annexure-A PIS requirements for buses and coaches.

One system for every sign on the bus

ITMS (Intelligent Transport Management System) is an LED Destination Board System with synchronized voice, designed for all categories of buses and coaches, including mini and midi buses. A single driver controller runs the front, side, rear, and inside signs together, so what passengers see outside matches what they see and hear inside.

Passengers know the route

Clear destination boards visible in fixed, scrolling, and flashing modes.

Passengers hear the next stop

GPS-triggered announcements in up to three languages.

Operators stay in control

Route changes and messages can reach the bus from the back office.

What ITMS does on every route

Ten functions, all driven from one controller

Route programming

Load route files onto the controller and pick a route from it. Supports a minimum of 75 routes, UP and DOWN (150 destinations), on the front, side, and rear signs, with easy sorting of routes.

GPS next-stop display

Next-stop display on the inside sign with synchronized voice announcement, for a minimum of 75 stops on each route.

Three-language announcements

Display and announce up to three languages in sequence, for example English, then Hindi, then the local language.

Announcement timing

Trigger display and voice before arrival, on arrival, and after departure at each stop.

Manual override

If GPS fails, the driver can continue the same functions manually through the controller.

Custom graphics and messages

Show customized graphics, including special signs such as PWD-enabled bus and Ladies special. The driver can play pre-recorded messages by selecting 1–9 on the controller.

Driver and conductor ID

Display driver and conductor ID once between stops on the inside sign.

Emergency stop request

A passenger presses an emergency switch placed anywhere in the bus. The inside sign shows a “stop” message and a buzzer near the driver sounds.

GPS clock and default messages

The inside sign can show a GPS-based clock or default messages.

Power-failure memory

The sign retains its last message through a power failure without reloading it from the controller.

Four controller tiers, (a) to (d)

IS 16490:2016, clause 4.3.1 lists the tiers as (a) to (d). The names below are our own friendly labels.

Tier A · cl. 4.3.1 (a)
Basic

Destination boards without voice and without GPS compatibility.

Tier B · cl. 4.3.1 (b)
Voice + GPS

Synchronized voice and display on the inward-facing board, with in-built GPS or GPS compatibility.

Tier C · cl. 4.3.1 (c)
Voice + GPS + two-way

Everything in B, plus two-way communication with the control centre through an add-on device.

Tier D · cl. 4.3.1 (d)
Full ITS compliant

Complete Intelligent Transport System compliance. Adds Ethernet and CAN interfaces.

Common to every controller (cl. 4.3.2)

  • Shows different messages on each sign: front, rear, side, and inner.
  • Memory sized to customer specification.
  • Automotive grade, rated −25 °C to +85 °C, with conformal-coated PCBs.
  • Alphanumeric controller display with a keypad of at least four keys.

Inside sign and content

The inward-facing sign is at least 800 mm × 100 mm, with a minimum of 16 rows and 112 columns (cl. 4.2.2). Content is alphanumeric with graphic capability in English, Hindi, and other local languages, in fixed, scrolling, and flashing modes, with a fixed route number as per customer requirement. It shows two lines of English and one line of local language with adjustable line heights, and can switch to a single line during next-stop announcements. Display and voice use Microsoft fonts (or other fonts as specified in the tender) through a Windows-based software package.

LED quality (Annexure-A, draft)

  • UV-resistant, diffused lens of 4 mm minimum, or SMT PLCC2 standard package.
  • Viewing angle 120° horizontal, 60° vertical.
  • Non-multiplexed constant-current drive, typical intensity 400–700 mCd at If = 20 mA; or multiplexed (max 4:1) at 950–1150 mCd at 20 mA.
  • Inside sign: 45° all around, minimum 40 mcd, dominant wavelength 591–595 nm (amber).
  • Front, side, and rear signs: lightweight structure with toughened glass fixed with UV-resistant adhesive. Inside sign: poly glass, acrylic, or toughened glass.

Built around the Indian standard for bus LED destination boards

IS 16490:2016

Covers the construction, performance requirements, and test methods for LED Destination Board Systems for all categories of buses and coaches, including mini and midi buses.

Draft Amendment No. 2 (Feb 2020)

Doc. TED 28(15407) proposes Annexure-A, adding functional requirements for PIS boards. Status: draft.

Status classes and testing

Functional status classes A to E (cl. 4.5) describe how a device behaves during and after electromagnetic exposure. Class A means every function works as designed during and after exposure. Type tests prove conformity with the standard. Acceptance tests are run on samples from a lot of 50 LDBS of the same type, design, and manufacture.

Questions from procurement teams

Which bus types does ITMS support?

All categories of buses and coaches, including mini and midi buses.

What voltage does it run on?

12 V or 24 V d.c.

What if GPS stops working?

The driver can run the same display and announcement functions manually from the controller.

Can the signs show different messages?

Yes. The front, rear, side, and inside signs can each show a different message.

What happens during a power cut?

Signs keep the last message in memory and show it again when power returns.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, and other local languages, with up to three played in sequence.

Can we update routes remotely?

With the two-way communication tiers (C and D), yes.

How many routes and stops can it hold?

At minimum 75 routes (UP and DOWN) and 75 stops on each route.
